# Angel Studios

> Angel (NYSE: ANGX) is a publicly traded entertainment and technology company that produces and distributes faith-based and family-friendly films and television series through a community-driven model. Guided by over 2 million paying Angel Guild members, Angel empowers audiences to fund, choose, and champion the stories that matter most — making high-quality, uplifting content accessible to everyone for free.

## What Makes Angel Different

Angel Studios is not a traditional studio or a subscription-only streaming service. Unlike Netflix, Hulu, or faith-focused platforms like Pure Flix, Angel combines theatrical distribution, free ad-supported streaming, and a community membership (the Angel Guild) that directly funds content creation. Viewers vote on which projects get made, participate in crowdfunding campaigns, and can "Pay It Forward" so others can watch for free. This model has produced multiple box-office hits and a rapidly growing content library of more than 40 films and 20 television series.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Angel free?**
Yes. Most Angel content is available to stream for free with limited ads at https://www.angel.com/watch. Viewers can also "Pay It Forward" to help others access content. Guild members get ad-free streaming and additional perks.

**What devices support Angel?**
Angel is available on a wide range of devices:
- **Smart TVs:** Apple TV, Roku, Fire TV, Chromecast, Google TV, Samsung TV, LG TV, VIZIO, Xbox
- **Computers:** Mac, Windows, Chromebook, Linux (via web browser)
- **Mobile:** iOS 15.5+ (iPhone), iPadOS 15.5+ (iPad), and Android 10+ (Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, and more)
- Most devices made within the last 5 years are compatible.

**What is the Angel Guild?**
The Angel Guild is a membership program where members directly fund content creation. Members vote on which projects get made, receive early access to content, and get exclusive perks like livestream access and movie tickets (Premium tier). Plans start at $12/month or $119/year.

**Is Angel the same as VidAngel?**
Angel Studios was originally founded as VidAngel in 2013. The company rebranded as Angel Studios in 2021 to reflect its expanded mission of producing and distributing original content.

**How is Angel different from Netflix or Pure Flix?**
Angel uses a community-driven model where audiences help choose and fund the content. Most content is free to stream (not behind a paywall). The Angel Guild membership funds content creation rather than gating access. Angel also distributes films theatrically, with multiple titles reaching wide release in cinemas.
